Career Opportunities as a Supply Chain Manager
Supply chain managers ensure that every link in the value chain runs smoothly, ethically, and cost-effectively. They oversee
logistics operations
, manage relationships with suppliers and distributors, and optimize processes using the latest technologies and best practices. Across industries – from e-commerce and manufacturing to healthcare and technology – supply chain experts are highly sought after. This growing demand translates to strong career prospects and exciting opportunities worldwide.
In-demand job titles
for graduates may include:
Global Logistics Manager
Humanitarian Logistics Manager
Operations Manager
Procurement Manager
Purchasing Manager
Risk Analyst
Supply Chain Consultant
Supply Chain Manager
and more.
The Program
Program: Master in Global Logistics and Supply Chain Management (MSc)
Start Date: Every September
Our Master in Global Logistics & Supply Chain Management (MSc) equips you with the skills needed to excel in one of the most dynamic and rewarding management fields. Choose between the Standard Track (2 years) or the Fast Track (1.5 years), depending on your background and career goals.
Key courses in this program include:
Global Logistics Strategies
Supply Chain Analytics
Sustainability in Supply Chains

vibrant, cosmopolitan city
with a rich trading history and a welcoming international community. As Germany’s second-largest metropolis and a major
